Climbing in Valtellina

Posted on Aug 12, 2024 DRAFT

While searching for info in English about climbing in my home area, i literally found nothing. Thus, I decided to write this little guide as a tool for foreigners to explore this beatiful climbing playground. Secondly, all italian guides often require prior knowledge of the area. I will try to make this guide as naive as possible.

Introduction

Valtellina is a valley located in the very top north of Italy, bordering with Switzerland. It offers an extensive area for any mountaneering sports, it is largely undocumented for foreigners. In fact, I heard only a few times any other language than Italian while climbing here (not counting local dialect :). Climbing here spans between technical granite slabs, steep limestone, traditional and sport routes for any style. All within 1h of driving from the main town Sondrio, and away from the big crowds of more famous places like Dolomites or Mont Blanc area.

I will divide climbing in Valtelina in different chapters and areas.

Val Masino e Val di Mello

This is a true gem of climbing, and probably the most famous point of intrest. Val di Mello is a valley stretching sideways from Valtellina, it offers amazing grinite rock. Sometimes called “Little Yosemity” or “University of Granite”.

Italian bouldering was born here around the end of 70s, some local youngsters started climbing without rope short routes to just have fun. Totally different compared to summit oriented alpinist of the time.

Bouldering developed through the years and peaked with Mello Blocco, an international competition that saw as partecipats figure like Adam Ondra and Stefano Ghisolfi. However, it doesn’t stop to bouldering: Val Di MEllo offers also extensive sport, trad climbing and even big walls. After pandemic, Mello Blocco was not organized for a couple of years. Local authorities saw also the increase of tourists as a threat to the environment and decided to keep the valley away from the big crowds, Mello Blocco is now still organized but in smaller form.
